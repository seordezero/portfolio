Year: 2026
Category: Audiovisual
Title: Secciones de Sonido
Subtitle: un experimento en tres tiempos
Collaborators: Mariano Gómez Luque

Audiovisual exhibition piece using Suno AI, Demucs stem separation, and Grasshopper/Rhino to computationally analyze and visualize three musical compositions.

The pipeline reads MP3 stems, extracts waveform and pitch data using librosa's pYIN algorithm via a custom Python script, and drives 3D mesh deformations synchronized to a time slider.
