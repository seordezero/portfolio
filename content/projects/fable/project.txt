Year: 2020
Category: Design
Title: faBle
Subtitle: self-assembling CNC furniture
Collaborators: Moisés Lázaro Gonzáles
Link: https://www.difable.xyz/

faBle started during the pandemic to offer a solution to the increasing demand for furniture that could easily adapt regular house spaces into remote working stations.

We wanted to design interesting and complex self-assembling furniture that we could produce locally with a CNC in a small workshop. We dedicated a lot of time to creating unique designs and getting the most benefits out of this unfamiliar technology.
