Year: 2021
Category: Design
Title: Foldable Breakfast Table
Subtitle: furniture for a 25 m² apartment
Collaborators: Carolina Rivera

This set of one table and two chairs was part of a bigger project that included the whole interior design of a 25-square-meter apartment that would be mainly rented for Airbnb. The place would only consist of a bed that merged into a closet with cabinets for basic storage and a desk; the terrace only had a small bar and a bench, and there was a preexisting kitchenette and bathroom design.

Therefore, we wanted to create a piece of furniture that could turn this minimal and ultra-efficient design into something cozier and more homelike.
