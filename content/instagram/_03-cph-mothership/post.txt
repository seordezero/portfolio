Proyecto: cph-mothership

CPH Mothership — X-Urban Studio, IAAC (2023)

A counter-proposal for Lynetteholm, Copenhagen. Taking Koolhaas' BIGNESS literally: the envelope separates from the organs, and modules from three experimental precedents assemble into a system of systems. Connections tested with Monoceros for Grasshopper.

With Mu-Huai Liu, Aikaterini Marinaki and Yang Xiao.

#architecture #urbanism #bigness #computationaldesign #grasshopper #iaac
