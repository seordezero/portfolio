Jirón Tumbes — studio restoration, Barranco (2021)

An interior courtyard in an old adobe house, turned into a loft studio with a mezzanine for artists. A new wood frame contains and revives the existing adobe walls: it carries the new roof while bracing the old structure.

With Carolina Rivera.
