A Room for Archeologists and Kids — PUCP × ETH Zürich (2018)

A pavilion at Pachacamac, the most important prehispanic centre on the Peruvian coast, designed and built on site with Tom Emerson's studio. Conical foundations filled with sand to keep off any undiscovered remains, and an adobe floor laid in a pattern taken from quincha weaving.

Dezeen Awards 2019 — Small Building of the Year.
